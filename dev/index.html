TryIt.jl

An ephemeral-workspace manager for Julia developers. tryit spins up a dated scratch directory, drops you into it, and lets you clone repositories or take a git worktree of the repo you're in without polluting your working tree.

TryIt.jl is a functional mirror of Tobi Lütke's try — also reimplemented in C as tobi/try-cli and in Rust as try-rs — with a TUI built on Tachikoma.jl.
